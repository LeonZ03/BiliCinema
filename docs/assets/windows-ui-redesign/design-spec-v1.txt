Interaction specification:
- Home accepts share text, URLs and BV IDs; clear x resets the whole input;
  clipboard button pastes. Join opens a compact dialog with input, clear/paste,
  join action and an inline error. Creating/joining displays staged progress.
- Account is an avatar popover/page: avatar, display name, UID with copy icon,
  login status, switch account, check updates, sign out. Logged out shows QR login.
- Viewing shows only essential playback state; technical diagnosis is on demand.
- Fullscreen chat uses an overlay: chat icon or right-side click opens; outside
  click, x or Escape closes; video geometry and playback do not change.
- Incoming messages briefly preview on the right even with chat closed.
- Guest controls do not override the host timeline; local volume and quality stay
  available. Clear user-visible recovery action for media errors.
- Tooltips explain icon-only controls; Tab focus remains visible; Enter sends
  chat, Escape first dismisses overlays and then exits fullscreen.
